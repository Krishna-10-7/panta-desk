# Panta Desk — pitch script

Target: 1 minute 45 seconds to 2 minutes, including the introduction. Replace the brackets with truthful details. Keep the introduction to about 15 seconds and rehearse before recording; the current submission portal limits this pitch to 2 minutes.

## Spoken script

Hi, I'm **[your name]**. **[One short, true sentence about your background and why you are presenting this project.]**

Panta Desk is a research workspace for people comparing prediction-market questions.

Two markets can sound similar while having different closing dates, resolution rules, or evidence sources. Looking at a price alone can hide those differences.

Panta Desk brings those terms together. You can browse Panta markets, inspect the exact question and supplied resolution information, and compare up to three markets side by side. You can save a watchlist, record review notes, and export a Markdown research brief.

The server reads Panta's categories, catalog, market details, and recent trades. The API key stays on the server. The interface shows the data source and retrieval time, distinguishes sample and sandbox data, and labels resolved outcome values separately from observed share prices. It does not turn those prices into verified forecasts.

The prototype requires no wallet connection. Notes stay in the browser, and data updates when requested rather than streaming continuously. It was built with AI assistance.

The next step is to test the comparison workflow with prediction-market researchers: can they identify differences in market terms, and is the exported brief useful? User adoption and pricing remain unvalidated.

The goal is a clearer, portable review of a market's question and evidence before someone decides how to participate.
